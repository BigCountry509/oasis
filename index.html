Catch test and tip wear

Run water through one tip for a full minute into a jug, or catch 15 seconds and multiply by four. Compare what you caught against what the tip is rated to flow. A tip more than 10 per cent over is worn out and is putting on more than you think.

Tank and acreage

How the numbers are worked out

Boom sprayer

GPM per tip = (GPA × MPH × tip spacing in inches) ÷ 5940

For a band, put the band width in place of the tip spacing.

Air blast sprayer

Total GPM = (GPA × MPH × row spacing in feet) ÷ 495

495 is for spraying both sides of the machine in one pass. Use 990 when you only spray one side per pass and drive both row middles.

Pressure and flow

Flow at any pressure = rating × √(pressure ÷ 40)

Every TeeJet capacity number is the flow in GPM at 40 PSI. Because flow follows the square root of pressure, four times the pressure only doubles the flow, which is why pressure is for small trims and a tip change is for real changes.

Where the data comes from

  • Droplet size classes for boom tips: TeeJet LI-TJ420 droplet size data, ISO 25358 standard, 15 inch tip spacing chart.
  • Air blast cone tips: TeeJet catalog CAT52-US, air blast nozzle section.
  • Air blast calibration constants and canopy volume split: university extension orchard sprayer calibration guidance.

Droplet classifications are published at set pressures and against a specific standard, and TeeJet revises them. Always check the chart on the box, and always confirm the tip against the product label before you spray.

The label is the law. This tool does the arithmetic and reads the published tip charts, it does not replace the pesticide label or a catch test. Confirm every setup against the current product label and measure your actual output before you spray.
